## Community palettes (third-party, excluded)

The 125 palettes under `community/` (`category: custom` in `index.json`) are
ports of base16 schemes collected by the
[tinted-theming](https://github.com/tinted-theming) project. They are
third-party works, not authored by xscriptor, and are **not licensed under
AGPL-3.0-or-later**.

- Each palette remains attributable to its original author. The full per-palette
  table is in [community/README.md](community/README.md); every palette file
  also keeps an `author` field.
- `Seti UI` is marked unknown because the upstream scheme declares no author;
  its author can claim, correct, or remove it under the same policy below.
- The upstream collections are MIT unless an individual scheme states
  otherwise. The schemes remain © their authors. This repository does not

- Names and trademarks belong to their respective authors/projects; no
  affiliation or endorsement is implied.
- They are included only as color references/ports.

## Removal and correction requests

Any community palette can be corrected, replaced, or removed at any time,
without notice, including immediately at the request of its author or rights
holder. To request a change or removal, contact x@xscriptor.com or open an
issue in `equisdots/palettes` with the palette slug/file.

Palettes created by users at runtime (`category: user`) are user data, are not
part of this repository or its license, and remain with their creators.
